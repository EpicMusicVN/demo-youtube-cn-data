## E01: Logos

### Old & New Comparison

The EMVN logo was updated in January 2020 to reflect our updated service design system and brand iconography style.

**Key Updates:**
- Maintained combination of simple, modern wordmark with stylized "E" symbol
- Refined ratios between letters of EMVN
- Systemized proportions with reasonable spacing
- **Important:** The old EMVN branding is no longer in use

### Lock-up Ratio & Clear Space

The primary logo is a key component of our identity and the preferred version for all materials and communications.

**Spacing Guidelines:**
- **Clear Space (Blue area):** Must be kept free of other elements
- **Safe Zone (Grey padding):** Additional buffer area
- **Alignment (Magenta):** Indicates type and element alignment and boundaries
- **Minimum Clear Space:** Defined by measurement circle (Yellow line) equal to the height of lowercase letters (cap-height). Width equals height.

### Sizing

Establishing minimum sizes ensures logo impact and legibility is not compromised.

**Digital Applications:**
- Full logo: Minimum 70px
- Symbol only: Minimum 21px

**Print Applications:**
- Full logo: Minimum 20mm (0.8in)
- Symbol only: Minimum 6mm (0.24in)

*Note: Print allows fractionally smaller sizes due to higher resolution (300dpi vs 72dpi for digital)*

### Exclusion Zone

**Impact Area:**
- An area around the logo defined as an impact area
- No other elements should be positioned within this zone

**Optimum Impact Area:**
- If the symbol "E" is the ratio for logo, then "x" is the verĐọctical and horizontal measurement of the symbol
- This "x" measurement defines the exclusion zone on all sides

**Examples:**
- ❌ NO: Other logos (e.g., Facebook) too close to EMVN logo
- ✅ YES: Both logos have enough room and don't compete
- ❌ NO: Copy line too close to logo
- ✅ YES: Copy has appropriate spacing (exclusion zone is minimum)

### Monochrome & White Out

**Monochrome:**
- All elements are black
- Only use against light background colors with enough contrast for legibility

**White Out:**
- Symbol and text inverted to white
- Only use against strong, dark background colors with sufficient contrast

### Incorrect Usage

The logo appearance must remain consistent. The logo should not be misinterpreted, modified, or added to.

**DO NOT:**
- ❌ Apply a gradient to the logo
- ❌ Change the font of logo
- ❌ Distort or warp the logo in any way
- ❌ Change the relative sizes of logo elements
- ❌ Outline or create a keyline around the logo
- ❌ Change the logo color or tone outside specified colors
- ❌ Change the type spacing of the logo
- ❌ Rotate the logo

### Logo Placement

The logo can only be placed in **five locations** to maintain consistency:

1. **Top left corner**
2. **Top right corner**
3. **Centered**
4. **Bottom left corner**
5. **Bottom right corner**

*Remember to respect the logo exclusion zone when placing in corners*

**Rules:**
- Colors must not clash
- Maintain level of contrast between logo, typography, and background

6. Place logo in one of five designated positions only
